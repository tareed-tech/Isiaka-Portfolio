02 · The profile

Aboutme

Student by programme, builder by practice. Where I studied, what I am aiming for, the tools I use and what I do when I step away from them.

I study Computer Science at Miva Open University and work as a product developer under the name Swift Tech. The two feed each other: coursework gives me the fundamentals, and client work gives me somewhere to apply them the same week.

Most of my projects start as a conversation about a problem rather than a list of features. I map the data first, then the flows, then the interface, and only then start building. That habit is what the UI/UX diploma is sharpening.

I work mostly in Bubble.io and Lovable with Supabase underneath, and I integrate AI services such as Claude, OpenAI, ElevenLabs and HeyGen into systems that carry real users and real payments.

aspirations

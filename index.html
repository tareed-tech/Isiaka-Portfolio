I build web products for a living while studying for my degree in Computer Science at Miva Open University. Most of my client work runs on Bubble.io and Lovable with Supabase behind it.

I handle the whole path: data model, workflows, interface, payments, testing, launch, and the support that comes after. A good share of it has artificial intelligence inside, from speech and on-screen avatars to language models carrying real traffic, which is a very different problem from making a demo work once.

Studying web technologies formally gives me the fundamentals underneath the tools I use every day. That is why this site is written by hand in plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

Health tech · Live

CPRehearsal

The examiner that never sleeps.

A rehearsal platform for medical case exams. A candidate runs a session in text, by voice, or face to face with an on-screen AI examiner, then receives a scored report as a PDF by email. I designed and built it end to end, including live payments, a custom domain and a 74-condition test pass before launch.
